Every movement begins with a moment of clarity.

For us, that moment came in 2019, when a group of driven Black and Brown men — most of us connected through Morgan State University — made a commitment to invest not just in markets, but in one another. What started as a modern take on a traditional “sou-sou” has grown into a dynamic investment club rooted in strategy, brotherhood, and purpose.
