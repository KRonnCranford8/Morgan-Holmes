Our Story

Morgan & Holmes, LLC

Est. May 1, 2019

Morgan & Holmes LLC was born from the vision of 20 Black and Brown men — most of whom met at Morgan State University — who believed in the power of collective economics and long-term investment. Inspired by the West African tradition of the “sou-sou” (a rotating savings and credit association, or ROSCA), we sought to modernize that model with a twist: instead of rotating the payout to individuals, we would pool our resources to invest — together, strategically, and intentionally.

In May 2019, Morgan & Holmes officially launched — not just as an investment club, but as a movement rooted in trust, legacy, and shared purpose. Over the past six years, we’ve grown stronger, leaner, and more focused. From 20 founding members, we now operate with a core of 12 active members and one trusted advisory investor, covering five states: Connecticut, New York, Maryland, Georgia, and Texas.

We’ve executed multiple private investments, built a diversified stock portfolio, and are now expanding aggressively into real estate opportunities. Our model remains true to our roots: powered by community, driven by strategy, and grounded in a deep commitment to Black excellence and financial empowerment.

🌍 Our Mission

To create self-sustainability within African-American and minority communities through investing and financial literacy.

🔭 Our Vision

To increase generational wealth amongst African-Americans and minorities — one investment, one project, and one community at a time.

At Morgan & Holmes, we don’t just move money — we move with intention. Our journey is only beginning.
