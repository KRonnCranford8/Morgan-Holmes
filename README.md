# Morgan & Holmes

Website for Morgan & Holmes LLC, a private member-based investment collective founded in 2019.

**Live:** [www.morganandholmes.com](https://www.morganandholmes.com)

## Problem

The existing Squarespace site was costing money for a platform-managed template, had a homepage with two competing calls to action and no clear trust signal above the fold, and needed to move to free static hosting without losing any of its content, tone, or the group's intentionally low-publicity philosophy (the club doesn't actively recruit or publicize itself).

## Role

Co-founder of Morgan & Holmes LLC and product owner on the migration: full site rebuild, a conversion audit of the existing page flow, and domain/DNS configuration. Directed and prompted every build and design decision using AI-assisted development; I owned the product thinking, the architecture, and every implementation call, the AI was the tool, I was the one deciding what got built and why.

## Product Decisions

- **Ran a conversion audit on the homepage** and found two equal-weight, competing CTAs ("Our Vision" and "Meet the Team"), neither of which was the actual action the business wanted a visitor to take. Reduced it to one clear primary action ("Get in Touch") and one secondary path.
- **Added an above-the-fold trust signal** (est. 2019, 12 active members, 5 states) so a first-time visitor gets legitimacy signals before scrolling, a common gap on member-based organization sites.
- **Deliberately did not add** urgency language, fake scarcity, exit-intent popups, or newsletter capture, a default CRO playbook that would have worked against a brand whose entire philosophy is discretion over broad recruitment. Matching the tactics to the actual brand strategy, not defaulting to generic growth-hacking patterns.
- **Migrated the full page set** (Home, Our Story, Our Team, Start a Club, Blog, Donate) from Squarespace to a plain HTML/CSS/JS static build with zero framework overhead, cutting the platform cost to zero.
- **Handled full domain and DNS migration** from Squarespace's hosting to GitHub Pages with a custom domain, with zero downtime on the switch.

## Result

A fully live, zero-hosting-cost static site with a clearer, more intentional homepage funnel and every page carried over from the original platform.
